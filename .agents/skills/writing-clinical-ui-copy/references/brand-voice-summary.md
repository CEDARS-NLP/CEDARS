# MSK Brand Voice — Digital Interface Summary

## Brand Character: The Dedicated Difference-Maker

Someone who:
- Works relentlessly to make a difference
- Makes progress personal
- Sees a way forward when others can't

In digital clinical interfaces, this character shows up as:
- Calm, competent guidance
- Language that respects the user's time and emotional state
- Clarity that builds trust

## Core Brand Platform

**Big brand idea:** Unstoppable progress, made personal

We drive the greatest advancements in cancer science and care to make the difference for you.

**Brand pillars:**
- **Research and care, united** — World-class ideas constantly flow between lab and clinic so our care is science-based and our research is patient-centered.
- **Our all, for you** — We leave no stone unturned in supporting you every step of your journey. Together, we embrace the highs and lows, giving you the confidence to face each moment with us by your side.
- **Outcomes, redefined** — The best outcome means your best outcome. We understand your unique needs and hopes for the future to go beyond the best clinical outcome and chart a clear path forward.

## Tone Spectrum

We dial elements up or down depending on the audience, the message, and the moment — but we are always the dedicated difference-maker.

- We show **more restraint** in functional moments (e.g., pre-surgical brochure, system UI)
- We let **personality show through** in most communications (e.g., newsletters)
- We show **more emotion** in moments that demand passion (e.g., giving appeals)

**For digital clinical interfaces, default to the restrained end of this spectrum.**

## Communication Intents

### Introduce
**Goal:** Expand awareness of who we are and what we offer
**We want people to feel:** Aware of MSK and our expertise

Signature tactics:
- **Start with what matters** — Consider your audience and what they need at this moment; lead with either emotion or hard facts, letting the other follow
- **Zoom in on passion** — Zoom in on dedicated individuals to make an impact with real stories
- **Leverage our legacy** — Elevate key details from our storied history that show longstanding expertise
- **Emphasize community** — Choose collective phrasing and inclusive language to acknowledge our connection to the many groups we serve

### Educate
**Goal:** Share our expertise, near and far
**We want people to feel:** Clear about the subject matter

Signature tactics:
- **Real details, real life** — Tap into details, even small ones, to add dimension to people, stories, and processes
- **Ask and answer** — Use a question-and-answer format to simplify the process of receiving a lot of detail
- **Why this is (or isn't) important** — Candidly explain why somebody should care, or why they may not need to
- **Speak person to person** — Use close-in, conversational phrasing as if speaking one-to-one

### Attract
**Goal:** Encourage potential patients, employees, and donors to come to MSK
**We want people to feel:** Impressed by our organization

Signature tactics:
- **Show our relentlessness** — Use energetic, driven language to show our relentless nature
- **We've seen it all** — Lean into our experience to make people feel confident
- **Soft confidence** — Speak to credentials in a way that's relatable and understated, elevating personal passion above all
- **Celebrate partnership** — Spotlight partnerships in a way that shows their value

### Welcome
**Goal:** Make people feel like they are in the right place
**We want people to feel:** Confident in their decision

Signature tactics:
- **Safe space** — Let people know MSK is a place for everyone, regardless of background or situation
- **Warm welcome** — Show up with warmth, empathy, and a sense of comfort (for patients) or genuine enthusiasm and personal support (for everyone)
- **Plan your unique path** — Lay out steps clearly and sequentially so people feel confident on a personalized path

### Support
**Goal:** Help people through issues, even when the stakes are highest
**We want people to feel:** Comforted we have their back

Signature tactics:
- **Relentlessly reassure** — Make it clear we're going to do all we can to support, look out for, and follow up
- **Avoid assumptions** — Show empathy without assuming we know what someone is feeling or thinking
- **Balanced optimism** — Infuse optimism and confidence into realities we face, without being unrealistic
- **Humanize directions** — Simplify and humanize the way we lay out directions, like we would explain it to a friend

### Follow-up
**Goal:** Stay connected to those whose lives we've played a role in
**We want people to feel:** Considered and important

Signature tactics:
- **Enduring care** — Our care doesn't stop when treatment stops; show we are always a resource with willing and helpful language
- **Small steps, big picture** — Connect big-picture goals to the small, tactical ways we're making them happen
- **Simple, engaging CTAs** — When asking people to do something, keep it direct and tangible
- **Embrace individual journeys** — Stay engaged, showing genuine enthusiasm and interest in helping people forge their path

### Request
**Goal:** Seek out the resources that help fulfill our mission
**We want people to feel:** Informed of their impact

Signature tactics:
- **Elevate the benefit** — Highlight real effects on people's lives to emphasize our commitment
- **Show gratitude** — Give sincere, heartfelt thanks with no additional asks or strings attached
- **Be a pioneer** — Lean into open-ended, hopeful language that aligns with our pioneering spirit

## Communication Best Practices

- **Active voice** — "We pursue groundbreaking cures" not "Groundbreaking cures are pursued by MSK"
- **Start with the audience** — Who they are and how they might feel in the moment will guide our approach
- **Conversational, human phrasing** — Choose "us" instead of "the clinical team"; find ways to explain complex terms
- **Recognize the gravity of our subject** — Take our obligation seriously without resorting to dry, tired phrasing
- **Keep things concise and mix up sentence structure** — Together, these help keep our audience engaged
- **Cultural sensitivity** — Choose phrases that translate accurately without sacrificing clarity; avoid idioms that may be unclear or insensitive

## Tone Boundaries for Digital UI

- The brand voice adds warmth but never adds unnecessary words
- If applying a brand tactic would violate an editorial rule, the editorial rule wins
- Avoid marketing-level emotional language in functional UI
- Users should feel supported, never sold to
- Default to the restrained end of the tone spectrum for system-generated messages
- Reserve warmer, more expressive tone for onboarding flows, welcome screens, and support content where human connection matters most
